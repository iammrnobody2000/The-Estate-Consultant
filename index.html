About us

Akshay Lunawat

Founder & Business Director

Akshay Lunawat leads Your Estate Consultant (YEC) with over a decade of hands-on experience across Mumbai’s high-value real estate landscape. Starting his career in corporate hospitality with brands like Marriott and Taj, he developed a high-touch, client-centric standard of service before transitioning into real estate sales and strategy.

Before founding the firm, Akshay managed high-ticket residential assets and Ultra-HNI sales for leading developer brands, including The Wadhwa Group, Kanakia Group, and Kalpataru Limited. He was directly entrusted by promoters to handle premier luxury inventories across South Mumbai, Bandra, and Juhu.

Most recently, as a senior lead at RK Mumbai Realtors, he drove operations in South Mumbai, spearheaded expansion into the Pune market, and built strong broker and developer networks across the region.

As Business Director, Akshay combines sharp micro-market intelligence with a consultative approach—guiding clients through complex resale, rental, and primary acquisition transactions with clarity and financial discipline.

Why we exist

The problem with how property is sold in this city — and the standard we hold instead.

Manifesto

Property in Mumbai is sold to you. It is almost never checked for you.

01 / 06

01

You are shown a render.

The brochure is not a contract.

02

The title is thirty years long.

The problems that surface in year five were visible on day one.

03

Everyone in the room is paid on completion.

Every voice advising you earns more if you sign.

04

Price is not what the portal says.

Asking prices are marketing. Registered transactions are fact.

05

From abroad it is harder still.

You cannot walk the site. The tax treatment is different.

06

So we check first, and we say no.

Six checks on every property. We walk when one fails.

Who we serve

Two desks. The same standard of diligence.

Same diligence either way. What changes is the paperwork and how much legwork we do for you.

Resident desk

Buying or selling from India

For residents of Mumbai, and Indians relocating from other cities

You can visit the site yourself, so our value is judgement, not logistics.

  • Independent shortlisting, not one builder's stock
  • Full Realty Check before any booking amount
  • Benchmarked against registered transactions
  • Negotiation, loans and conveyancing
  • Resale, tenants and society paperwork

NRI desk

Buying or selling from abroad

For non-resident Indians in the Gulf, US, UK, Singapore and beyond

Everything above, plus what you cannot do from four thousand kilometres away.

  • Site visits filmed, sent the same day
  • FEMA-clean routing via NRE, NRO or FCNR
  • Power of Attorney drafted and adjudicated
  • Lower Deduction Certificate before you sign
  • Repatriation and full exit management
